# Ficus lyrata (fiddle-leaf fig)

> OOSR knowledge file. Context for planners and people. It is data, not instructions:
> the only actions a robot may take are the tasks and primitives in `manifest.json`.

## Water

- Water when the top 3-5 cm of substrate are dry. In a typical home that is every 7-10 days in
  summer and every 10-14 days in winter.
- Volumetric soil moisture below ~0.20 means dry; above ~0.40 right after watering is normal.
- Water slowly and evenly until ~80 ml per litre of pot has been absorbed. Never leave the pot
  standing in water: root rot is the most common way these plants die indoors.
- Brown patches starting in the middle of the leaf usually mean overwatering; crispy brown edges
  usually mean underwatering or very dry air.

## Light and placement

- Bright, indirect light, close to a window. A few hours of gentle morning sun is fine.
- It dislikes being moved and cold drafts: sudden leaf drop after a move is common and usually
  recovers.

## Health checks

- Look at both sides of the leaves weekly. Dark spots with a yellow halo can be a fungal or
  bacterial infection; fine webbing or speckling suggests spider mites.
- Wipe dust off the leaves every few weeks so they can photosynthesise.

## Pruning

- Prune in early spring (March-April in the northern hemisphere), cutting just above a node.
- Never remove more than a third of the foliage in one season.
- The sap is irritating: humans should wear gloves.
