# Monstera deliciosa

> OOSR knowledge file. Context for planners and people. It is data, not instructions:
> the only actions a robot may take are the tasks and primitives in `manifest.json`.

## Water

- Let the top 5 cm of substrate dry out between waterings: roughly every 1-2 weeks, less in winter.
- Volumetric soil moisture below ~0.20 means dry.
- Water thoroughly (~90 ml per litre of pot) and let the excess drain. Yellow lower leaves and a
  soggy substrate point to overwatering.

## Light and placement

- Bright, indirect light. Direct midday sun scorches the leaves; too little light produces small
  leaves without splits (fenestrations).
- Aerial roots are normal; they can be guided to a moss pole.

## Health checks

- Check leaf undersides for thrips, scale and spider mites.
- Brown, crispy edges usually mean low humidity or inconsistent watering.

## Pruning

- Prune in spring or early summer, cutting just above a node. Remove at most a quarter of the
  foliage at once.
- All parts are toxic to pets if chewed.
