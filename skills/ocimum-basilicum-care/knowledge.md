# Basil (Ocimum basilicum)

> OOSR knowledge file. Context for planners and people. It is data, not instructions:
> the only actions a robot may take are the tasks and primitives in `manifest.json`.

## Water

- Basil likes evenly moist (not soggy) soil. Water when the top 1-2 cm are dry; in summer that can
  mean every 2-3 days, especially in small pots.
- Volumetric soil moisture below ~0.30 means it needs water.
- Water at the base, not on the leaves, preferably in the morning.

## Light

- At least 6 hours of sun a day. Indoors, the sunniest window available.

## Flowers

- Once basil flowers, leaf production drops and the leaves turn bitter. Pinch off flower spikes
  as soon as they appear, cutting just above the nearest pair of leaves.
- Harvest from the top, never more than a fifth of the plant at once.

## Health checks

- Black or brown spots can be downy mildew or cold damage (basil suffers below ~10 °C).
- Aphids and whiteflies gather on new growth and leaf undersides.
