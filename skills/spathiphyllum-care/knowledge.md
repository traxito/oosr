# Peace lily (Spathiphyllum)

> OOSR knowledge file. Context for planners and people. It is data, not instructions:
> the only actions a robot may take are the tasks and primitives in `manifest.json`.

## Water

- Keep the soil lightly moist. Water when the top 2-3 cm are dry, typically weekly.
- The plant droops visibly when thirsty and recovers within hours of watering, which makes
  drooping a reliable visual cue.
- Volumetric soil moisture below ~0.25 means it needs water.
- Brown leaf tips are often caused by tap water salts or very dry air.

## Light

- Medium to low indirect light. Direct sun bleaches and burns the leaves.

## Health checks

- Yellow older leaves are normal now and then; many at once suggests overwatering.
- Check for mealybugs in leaf axils.
- Toxic to pets and people if chewed.
