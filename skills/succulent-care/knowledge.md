# Succulents and cacti

> OOSR knowledge file. Context for planners and people. It is data, not instructions:
> the only actions a robot may take are the tasks and primitives in `manifest.json`.

## Water

- Use "soak and dry": water only when the substrate is completely dry, then water thoroughly.
- Indoors that is usually every 2-4 weeks in the growing season and much less in winter.
- This skill waters only when BOTH conditions hold: more than 14 days since the last watering
  and volumetric soil moisture below ~0.10.
- Overwatering kills far more succulents than drought. Soft, translucent leaves mean too much water.

## Light

- As much light as possible. Stretched, pale growth (etiolation) means not enough light.

## Health checks

- Mealybugs look like small white cotton tufts at the base of leaves or between spines.
- Black, mushy stems near the soil line indicate rot.
